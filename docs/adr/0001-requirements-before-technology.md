# ADR-0001

## Title

Requirements Before Technology

---

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

A common mistake in software projects is choosing technologies before understanding the real business problem.

This often leads to unnecessary complexity.

---

## Decision

Architecture decisions must always be based on documented requirements.

Technology should only be selected after understanding the business needs.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Better architecture
- Better communication
- Less unnecessary complexity

### Negative

- More time spent analysing requirements before implementation
